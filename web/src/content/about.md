You enter, you play, you have fun. No need to bring anything.

ALL GEAR PROVIDED:
· Consoles & Games
· Unique setups (VR, simulators, etc.)
· Arcade cabinets
· Controllers & peripherals
· Screens & audio
· Network infrastructure
